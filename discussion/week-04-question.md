title: "Lasso with Identical Predictors"
author: kum2
---

Suppose two standardized predictors are exactly identical, and lasso assigns a positive coefficient to one and zero to the other. Would splitting that coefficient equally between them change the predictions or the lasso penalty? What does this imply about interpreting a zero coefficient as evidence that a predictor is unimportant?
